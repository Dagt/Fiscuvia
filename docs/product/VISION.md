# Product vision and positioning
## North star
**Fiscuvia — Todo tu despacho, una sola vía.** One operational workspace for Mexican accounting practices. Keep accounting systems (e.g., CONTPAQi) in place, connect evidence and workflows around them.

## Users
Independent accountants, small/medium accounting firms, firm administrators, supervisors, assistants and taxpayers invited to a client portal (post-MVP).

## Jobs to be done
- Know what is due, who owns it, what has been prepared, filed, paid or reviewed, and where the evidence lives.
- Track documents and renewal dates of e.firma/CSD **without custody of secrets in MVP**.
- Manage firm fees and collections independently of tax payments.
- Reduce scattered spreadsheets, messages and manual status updates.
- Later: import and synchronize CFDI and assist comparison with bank movements.

## Positioning
Operations/relationship/compliance tracking, **not** replacement for professional accounting, tax determination or filing. A digitally mature, easy-to-use product with proof of work, not just a dashboard of checkboxes.

## Differentiation hypotheses (not validated claims)
1. Extraordinary usability and monthly obligation matrix.
2. Evidence-first workflow with separation of filed vs paid states.
3. End-to-end requests and client portal.
4. Fees and firm profitability.
5. Import workflow from third-party accounting and CFDI tools.
Validate against current CONTPAQi Optimiza/competitors in customer interviews; do not assume feature absence.

## What success looks like
Qualitative: user knows today’s priorities in <2 minutes, can evidence a closed obligation, and can identify late document requests. Pilot metrics to validate: time to onboard taxpayer, on-time completion, overdue tasks, percent obligations with proof, hours spent per monthly close, activation and churn.

## Naming/legal note
Fiscuvia selected working brand; trademark and domain availability require separate official confirmation.
